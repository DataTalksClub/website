Frequent questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every video, homework and solution stays public — there is nothing to pay and nothing to upsell.

Can I join after a cohort starts?

Yes, while it is still running. Catch up on the recordings and submit whatever homework is still open.

How much time do I need?

Enough for the weekly videos plus hands-on practice. Most people do it alongside a full-time job — each course's own syllabus lays out its pace.

What should I know beforehand?

Comfort with Python and the command line goes a long way. Each course page lists its exact prerequisites.

Do I get a certificate?

To get a certificate you need to pass a project.

Which course should I start with?

Switching into data? Start with Data Engineering Zoomcamp or Machine Learning Zoomcamp. Already shipping models? Try MLOps Zoomcamp or LLM Zoomcamp.

Have more questions? Join the community Slack and ask away.
