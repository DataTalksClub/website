Defining them once, in the Studio base's own style block, is what keeps the two sections one surface instead of two that drift. A Studio Courses page therefore inherits the whole design 5a document (one inline stylesheet, no external CSS, the shared masthead/footer/script partials) and adds only what is its own: the breadcrumb trail back to Studio, the local-date script its deadline columns use, and its content block.
