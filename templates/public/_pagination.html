{% comment %} The one page-number control the public catalogues share (issue #178). It is drawn entirely from the design 5a primitives, so it needs no rule of its own on any page that includes it: the row is a `.filter-pills` row, which is the system's own "one row of controls where exactly one is current" shape, and the current page is marked with `aria-current="page"`, the state that primitive already fills ink for. The numbers wear the shared `.pagination-number` mark, the same lopsided hand-drawn circle the step discs and the play disc use, and an elided range is the quiet bare `.status-pill`, hidden from assistive technology because it names nothing a reader can go to. The two directions are the arrows, not the words: at the end of a list of numbers a glyph reads faster than a label, and the label a reader cannot see is the one that matters — each carries its direction and its destination page as its accessible name, and the arrow itself is hidden. Everything here was decided in `content/pagination.py`: the window, the labels and every URL, including the fact that the first page is the clean path and is never spelled with a page query. This template builds no URL and reads no query. A direction that leads nowhere is absent rather than present and disabled, so the row never offers a target that does not move.
