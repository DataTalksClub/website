Who these sessions are for

I made these sessions for developers in Berlin who already write code:

  1. You're job hunting, laid off or in a notice period.
  2. You have a job and want to learn Cursor, Claude Code or LLM APIs.

Any programming language works for the first two sessions, and Python helps for 7 December. Anyone can join from anywhere, though the job market and Agentur für Arbeit parts focus on Berlin.

If you've never written code, these sessions will move too fast. My free Zoomcamps are a better place to start.

Three Mondays, 17:00 to 18:00 Berlin time

  1. 01Monday 23 November

    AI Engineering vs Agentic Engineering

    AI engineering is building apps that use LLMs, like chatbots, search over documents or agents. Agentic engineering is using tools like Claude Code or Cursor to write and ship code faster.

    In the first 15 to 20 minutes I'll explain:

    • how they are different
    • what companies want for each
    • which one to pick if you are a backend, frontend or data person
    • how to get a Bildungsgutschein for your training

    Then I'll answer your questions.

  2. 02Monday 30 November

    Agentic Engineering: How I Build Software with Coding Agents

    Agentic engineering is using tools like Claude Code or Cursor to write most of the code, while you decide what to build and check the result.

    In the first 15 to 20 minutes I'll explain:

    • how my work changed since I started using agents
    • what I still do myself and where agents make mistakes
    • what companies expect from developers who use them
    • how to get a Bildungsgutschein for your training

    Then I'll answer your questions.

  3. 03Monday 7 December

    AI Engineering: How to Build Apps on Top of LLMs

    AI engineering is building products that use LLMs, like chatbots, search over your company's documents or agents that do tasks for users.

    In the first 15 to 20 minutes I'll explain:

    • what AI engineers build day to day
    • which skills from backend, frontend or data work you already have
    • what to learn first if you want to move into it
    • how to get a Bildungsgutschein for your training

    Then I'll answer your questions.

After the talk and Q&A I spend about ten minutes on the full-time program I'm planning for April 2027 and on how the Bildungsgutschein works. If you're not interested, you can leave before that part.
