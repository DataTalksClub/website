What a cohort week looks like

The same shape for every zoomcamp, from the community's own logistics docs.

Cost
Free, incl. certificate
Length
7–10 weeks
Weekly load
10–15 hours
Final project
2–3 weeks

Office hours and the occasional workshop stream live on the DataTalks.Club YouTube channel — no Zoom link, no separate tool, no signup, just open YouTube at the scheduled time. The certificate needs the project and its peer reviews; the weekly homework itself is optional, though it's what the leaderboard runs on.

“The practicality of DataTalks is the huge difference. … The issue with that university and others is that everything stays in a Jupyter notebook. You know how the algorithm works … but it never goes beyond the notebook. … The difference with DataTalks is that the goal is for the person to use it in real life.”
Dashel Ruiz Perez, ML Zoomcamp graduate (opens in a new tab)

What Slack is actually like

A few norms, straight from the community guidelines.

  • Don't ask to ask (opens in a new tab) — just post your question in the channel.
  • Copy-paste your error as text, in a code block — never a screenshot or a photo of your screen.
  • Built on mutual respect and kindness: judge the code, never the person asking.
