{% comment %} The wiki graph explorer: a ring of real wiki relations around a hub topic, drawn server-side as the no-JS fallback, which core/static/core/home_graph.js then turns into a walk (click a neighbour to re-centre on it). The homepage draws the full card (Back, Random topic, Open wiki page, the legend and the count line), its controls quiet text links like the compact card's: they are secondary actions inside a soft card, and 2px ink is for calls to action; the wiki hub's hero passes `compact=True` for the card without the chrome: a "Random topic" control and the "full graph" link in its top corner, and the drawing. Its rules are core/_wiki_graph_explorer_styles.html, which every page drawing this partial includes in its stylesheet. Context: `wiki_graph` (core.home_content.wiki_graph()), and on the full card `counts.wiki`. Pages load core/home_graph.js after it.
